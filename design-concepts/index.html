Operato — design concepts

Nine visual directions across the app's Overview and Ask Operato screens, built from the real information architecture (sidebar nav, KPI tiles, alerts, charts, Ask Operato). Same dummy data throughout so styles are directly comparable. Click a card to open it full-size.

Round 1 — original directions

Concept 1

Hearth — warm hospitality

Cream & terracotta, serif display headings, soft rounded cards. Boutique, human, food-forward.
Concept 2

Signal — dark, AI-native

Near-black with indigo/violet + cyan glow, glassmorphic cards, monospace data accents.
Concept 3

Clean Ops — minimal SaaS

Tight neutral-gray base with one confident emerald accent, dense tables, thin borders.
Concept 4

Citrus — vibrant startup

White base with orange→pink→purple gradient mesh, glassmorphic KPI tiles, bold rounded cards.

Concept 8 · after "LoopAI"

Reimagines the Ask Operato page itself as a chat thread with quick-prompt chips and a question-history sidebar, SQL transparency preserved.

Original synthesis

Concept 9 · mineRECOMMENDED

Meridian

Warm-neutral base with a confident teal/coral identity (not grayscale). AI is ambient everywhere via a sidebar launcher + global ⌘K bar, not just one nav item. Sparklines on KPI tiles, a comparison ghost-line on the revenue chart. Built on a plain sidebar+content shell so it scales cleanly to every module — tables, dialogs, forms.
