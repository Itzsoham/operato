Original synthesis

Concept 9 · mineRECOMMENDED

Meridian

Warm-neutral base with a confident teal/coral identity (not grayscale). AI is ambient everywhere via a sidebar launcher + global ⌘K bar, not just one nav item. Sparklines on KPI tiles, a comparison ghost-line on the revenue chart. Built on a plain sidebar+content shell so it scales cleanly to every module — tables, dialogs, forms.

Round 3 — purpose-built for restaurant admin

Concept 10 · Mise

Mise — service-aware operations

Warm paper + bay-leaf green with saffron as identity, and red held back so a stock-out is the only alarming thing on screen. Signature elements: a day-part rail (prep / lunch / lull / dinner / close) instead of a flat date range, and an ordinal order-state chip — colour plus a 5-tick meter, so status survives greyscale and glare. Ships a second density, Service mode, for floor tablets, where saffron is promoted to primary because on a dark ground it out-reads the green 8.4:1 to 1.4:1.
Concept 10 · palettes

Food-forward palette options

Five replacements for the bay-green identity — Tandoor, Masala, Terracotta & Olive, Wine & Copper, Chai & Cinnamon — each drawn from real food and kitchen materials and shown on the same dashboard fragment so they're directly comparable. Pick one and the concept + token spec get rebuilt in it.
Concept 10 · spec

Mise — design system

The full token set: warm-neutral ramp, brand and semantic palettes with computed WCAG ratios, the order-lifecycle system, a 6-colour chart ramp all ≥3:1, the dark Service-mode palette, type scale, spacing, radii, elevation — plus paste-ready shadcn CSS variables for globals.css and the next/font setup.
