What were our top 5 dishes by revenue this month?
10:15 AM
Butter Chicken led with ₹42,180 across 184 orders, followed by Paneer Tikka (₹31,900), Chicken Biryani (₹29,600), Masala Dosa (₹18,200) and Cold Coffee (₹9,700). Based on full data
How this was calculated ⌄ 
SELECT mi.name, SUM(oi.quantity * oi.price) AS revenue
FROM "OrderItem" oi
JOIN "MenuItem" mi ON mi.id = oi."menuItemId"
WHERE oi."restaurantId" = $1
  AND oi."createdAt" >= date_trunc('month', now())
GROUP BY mi.name ORDER BY revenue DESC LIMIT 5;
